QRP Transceiver Power Latch Circuit (Short Press ON, Long Press OFF)
Date: 2026-05-23

Purpose
- Very low current when off.
- Controlled (orderly) shutdown before power is removed.
- User behavior: short press POWER to turn on, long press POWER to turn off.

Parts
1) Q1: P-channel MOSFET high-side switch (AO3407A, IRLML6402, or similar)
2) Q2: N-channel MOSFET gate pull-down driver (2N7002, BSS138, or similar)
3) R1: 1M (Q1 gate to VIN, default OFF)
4) R2: 100R to 1k (series from button node to Q1 gate, optional protection)
5) R3: 100k (Q2 gate to GND pulldown)
6) C1: 100nF (Q1 gate to source/VIN for noise immunity)
7) SW1: Momentary push button (POWER)

Pin-by-Pin Wiring
1) VIN -> Q1 source
2) Q1 drain -> V_SYS (main system rail)
3) Q1 gate -> GATE_CTRL node
4) R1 from GATE_CTRL to VIN
5) C1 from GATE_CTRL to VIN
6) SW1 one side -> GND
7) SW1 other side -> GATE_CTRL (through R2)
8) Q2 drain -> GATE_CTRL
9) Q2 source -> GND
10) Q2 gate -> Pico POWER_HOLD GPIO (optional 1k series)
11) R3 from Q2 gate to GND

Operation
OFF state
- R1 pulls Q1 gate up to VIN, so Q1 is OFF.

Power ON (short press)
- Press SW1: GATE_CTRL pulled low.
- Q1 turns ON and powers V_SYS.
- Firmware quickly drives POWER_HOLD high.
- Q2 turns ON and keeps GATE_CTRL low after button release (latched ON).

Power OFF (long press)
- Firmware detects long press on POWER button.
- Firmware saves settings to flash.
- Firmware mutes outputs and disables clocks.
- Firmware drives POWER_HOLD low.
- Q2 turns OFF, R1 pulls gate high, Q1 turns OFF, V_SYS drops.

Suggested GPIOs for this project
- POWER button input: GP15 (already used in firmware)
- POWER_HOLD output: GP5 (recommended free pin)

Firmware timing suggestions
- Debounce: 20 to 30 ms (25 ms currently used)
- Long-press threshold: 1.5 to 2.0 s (1.5 s currently used)

Checks before bring-up
- Verify PMOS orientation (source to VIN, drain to V_SYS).
- Ensure PMOS Rds(on) is low enough for full load current.
- Keep GATE_CTRL traces short to reduce noise pickup.
- If startup is noisy, increase C1 up to 220nF and re-test button behavior.
